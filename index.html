Income

Net monthly amounts that actually reach you (after any salary deductions not listed as debts below).

Debts & Loans

Balance, annual rate (APR %), scheduled monthly payment, and optional fixed term in months. Paid off highest-rate first (avalanche); freed payments roll into the next debt.

Credit Cards

Revolving card balances: balance, APR %, fixed payment/mo, or dynamic min % (e.g. 5% of balance). Cards paid in full belong under Expenses.

Obligations

Fixed monthly commitments: rent, utilities, school fees, remittances, insurance.

Living Expenses

Variable monthly spending: food, transport, leisure — a rough average is enough.

Other Assets & Gratuity

Non-market assets that form part of your net worth: property equity, physical gold, end-of-service gratuity accrued, or defined pensions. Enter current value and expected annual growth %.

Investing & Cash

Your market engine: current invested balance, what you add monthly, and a long-run return assumption (be honest — 8% is a fair planning number, not a promise).
Your safety cushion: cash you hold today, yield it earns, and target runway in months before investing begins. Unallocated monthly remainder accumulates here.

      How it reads. Surplus = income − obligations − expenses − scheduled debt payments. Your "monthly to stocks" and "extra to debt" must fit inside that surplus; freed payments from finished debts roll into the next-highest-rate balance, and once every debt is gone the whole freed amount flows into cash savings. Compounding & In-month Timing. Interest accrues monthly on beginning balances; payments, annual raises, inflation step-ups, and investment contributions apply at the monthly compounding boundary. Cash investments only proceed when cash buffer is positive and meets the safety threshold. Currency. The selector changes display only — amounts are never converted between currencies; enter every figure in the same currency you pick. Privacy. Signed out, everything stays in this browser. Signed in, your numbers sync to your own private cloud account — readable only by you, protected by row-level security.
